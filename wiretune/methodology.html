TRANSPARENT ESTIMATES

Methodology

Every starting length and tuning action keeps its assumptions visible.

Starting geometry

Half-wave dipole, inverted-V, and end-fed half-wave active paths begin with 142.6464 divided by frequency in MHz, multiplied by the selected wire factor. A quarter-wave vertical radiator and each equal radial use 71.3232. A full-wave loop perimeter uses 306.324. These practical coefficients produce starting points, not guaranteed installed resonance.

Three-reading estimate

WireTune fits a local quadratic only when three ordered analyzer readings describe a convex curve with a plausible minimum inside the measured span. Duplicate frequencies, invalid SWR, a non-convex curve, an outside minimum, or an excessive predicted change produces no physical instruction.

Bounded first change

For a valid sweep, the inverse-frequency estimate compares the current active path with the target. The first physical change is capped at half the predicted difference and one percent of the active path. When the antenna is short, only reserve-unfolding or extension guidance is shown.

Field responsibility

Supports, height, nearby objects, feedline, matching hardware, conductor construction, ground, and weather can move the measured result. Remeasure after every change in the final deployed geometry. WireTune does not replace an analyzer, installation inspection, or applicable safety and licensing requirements.
