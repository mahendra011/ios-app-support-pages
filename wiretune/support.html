FIELD HELP

Support

Keep the measured antenna, its geometry, and its limits together.

Start from the physical wire

Choose a family, target frequency, units, and wire factor in Band. Shape the antenna around the supports you actually have. In Cut, treat the resonant marks as starting points and leave the shown reserve at the listed adjustment locations.

When no adjustment appears

WireTune needs three distinct, ordered frequency readings with a plausible local SWR minimum inside the measured span. Correct duplicated frequencies or readings below 1.0, or widen the analyzer sweep. Your entered values remain intact.

When the antenna reads short

WireTune recommends unfolding reserved wire or adding wire at the listed locations. It never recommends cutting an antenna whose estimated resonance is above the target.

Export recovery

If PDF creation is unavailable, the complete local project remains unchanged. Use the text fallback from Field Card and try PDF again later. Cancelling the system share sheet does not alter the project.

Deleting data

Delete requires confirmation. Confirmed deletion removes that build from the app. Uninstalling the app removes remaining local builds, subject to operating-system backup behavior.
