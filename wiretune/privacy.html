Privacy

WireTune is designed to complete its entire job without collecting or transmitting data.

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Data collection

WireTune does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, account, cloud-sync, crash-reporting, or billing SDK.

Local data

Project names, antenna family, target frequency, wire factor, support geometry, cut reserve, feedline notes, analyzer readings, and saved iterations are stored only in the app's local container.

Export and sharing

A PDF or text field card is created only after an explicit user action. It leaves the app only through the operating system share surface and the destination the user selects.

Permissions and network

WireTune requests no camera, microphone, location, Bluetooth, contacts, notification, or other protected permission. It has no service endpoint, and the Android app declares no Internet permission. This website is separate and uses no cookies or analytics.

Control

Individual builds can be deleted from the app after confirmation. Uninstalling the app removes its remaining local data, subject to operating-system backup settings.
