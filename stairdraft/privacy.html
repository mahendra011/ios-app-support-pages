StairDraft Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026.

StairDraft does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or network service.

Local project data

Measurements, planning limits, selected layouts, and notes stay in the app's local storage. StairDraft does not request your location, camera, photos, contacts, or microphone.

Exports

A PDF or project summary leaves StairDraft only when you use the operating system's share, print, or save flow. The service or destination you choose has its own privacy practices.

Deleting data

Delete a project inside StairDraft, clear the app's storage on Android, or uninstall the app. Removing the app deletes its remaining local project data unless your operating system restores an app backup.
