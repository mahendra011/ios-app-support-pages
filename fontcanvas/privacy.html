Privacy

Your typeface projects stay on your device unless you explicitly export them.

Effective date: 27 August 2026

Data collection

FontCanvas does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. It has no account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, cloud backend, or third-party data processor.

Local projects and files

Font projects and preferences are stored locally on your device. You may explicitly choose an SVG or FontCanvas project to import and explicitly choose where to share or save exported TrueType, SVG, project, and PDF files. FontCanvas processes those files on-device and does not upload them.

Permissions

FontCanvas does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, health, or payment permissions. System file pickers and share sheets show only when you request an import or export.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes the paid storefront download under its own privacy policy. FontCanvas does not receive or store payment-card details and offers no in-app purchases.

Children

FontCanvas is a general creative tool and is not directed specifically to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children because it collects no personal data.

Contact and changes

If data handling ever changes, this page and its effective date will be updated before the corresponding release. Email namanmahendra8909@gmail.com with privacy questions.
