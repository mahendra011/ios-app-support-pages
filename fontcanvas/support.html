Support

Practical help for drawing, proofing, and exporting your font.

Which tool should I use?

Select moves outline nodes. Pen places straight contour points. Freehand turns a drag into a closed outlined stroke. Rectangle and Oval add editable shapes. Pan moves only the viewport—it intentionally does not draw.

Why is export blocked?

Open Proof or Export and read the blocking validation issues. Every exportable glyph needs safe, non-empty closed geometry and the project needs valid font identity fields. Fix the listed issue and validate again.

What can I import?

FontCanvas accepts supported SVG paths and FontCanvas project archives chosen through the system file picker. Import only outlines you created or have permission to use.

Where are projects stored?

Projects remain in the app's local storage. Exports leave the app only when you choose a destination through the platform share or save interface.
