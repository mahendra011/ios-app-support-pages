CineBalance Privacy Policy

CineBalance does not collect personal data.

  • No account is required.
  • No backend service operated by the developer is used.
  • No analytics, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs are included.
  • Shot packages, camera settings, lens values, notes, and scout-frame records are stored locally on the device.
  • Camera access is used only for live local frame assist and in-app scout still capture. CineBalance does not record video, upload media, or analyze video.
  • Scout stills are saved inside the app container on the device and are not uploaded by CineBalance.
  • If you tap Fetch public forecast, CineBalance sends the latitude and longitude values entered in the app directly to the public Open-Meteo weather API. The developer does not receive, store, or process that request.
