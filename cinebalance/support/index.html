CineBalance Support

CineBalance is a local-first cinematography field computer for shot packages, frame assist, exposure matching, lens planning, media estimates, sun and lighting checks, and production slates.

For support, email namanmahendra8909@gmail.com.

Important

CineBalance provides planning estimates and field reference tools. It is not a calibrated exposure meter, weather authority, camera manufacturer tool, or safety certification product. Verify final exposure, media, rigging, weather, electrical, and lighting decisions with your camera, meter, crew, and manufacturer documentation.

Local Features

  • Camera preview and scout still capture stay local to the device.
  • Optional public forecast lookup uses manually entered coordinates and does not require an account or developer backend.
  • The app works offline except for the optional forecast request.
