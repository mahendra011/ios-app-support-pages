DishArcSupportPrivacyMethodology

Privacy

DishArc is designed to work without an account or backend.

Effective date: 24 August 2026

Data collection

DishArc does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, account, cloud-sync, or crash-reporting SDK.

Local data

Site name, coordinates, selected orbital slots, obstruction marks, sensor readings, and notes are stored only in the app’s local container. A PDF is created only after an explicit export action and is shared only through the system share sheet you control.

Location

On iPhone and iPad, foreground location is requested only after “Use current site.” Android version 1.0 uses manual coordinates and does not request location. No background location is used.

Camera and sensors

Camera is requested only after the Camera action. Preview frames are processed on device and are not transmitted or silently saved. Heading and motion readings stay on device.

Network access

The app has no service endpoint. Android declares no internet permission. The support and privacy website is separate and uses no cookies or analytics.

Control

Delete the active session in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its remaining local data, subject to operating-system backup settings.
