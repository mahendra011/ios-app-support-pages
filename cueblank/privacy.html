CueBlank Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026.

CueBlank does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Scripts and rehearsal data

Documents you select are processed on your device. Parsed scenes, role choices and rehearsal history are stored locally. Cue speech uses the operating system's on-device Text-to-Speech service. CueBlank does not upload your script.

Exports

A rehearsal summary leaves the app only when you explicitly use the operating system share or save flow. The destination you choose has its own privacy practices.

Deleting data

You can delete local CueBlank data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
