CUEBLANK

Private rehearsal from the cue.

CueBlank imports a scene you own, lets you repair the dialogue, hides your role and runs spoken cues entirely on your device.

Support

For help, email naman.mahendra@gmail.com. Include your platform and app version, but do not attach a script you do not want to share.

Import tips

Use a text-based PDF or plain-text file with dialogue formatted as ROLE: dialogue. Scanned image-only PDFs need text recognition before import.
