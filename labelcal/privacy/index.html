LABELCAL / PRIVACY

Your labels stay on your device.

LabelCal does not collect or transmit personal data.

What LabelCal stores

Printer and label-stock profiles, measurements, project details, artwork references, placements, and output history are stored locally in the app or in files you explicitly choose to save.

Files and photos

LabelCal opens artwork only after you select it with the system photo or document picker. It receives access to the selected item for the requested workflow and does not request broad access to your photo library or device storage.

Printing, saving, and sharing

When you print, save, or share, LabelCal hands the generated output to the operating system. Your selected printer service, file provider, or destination app operates under its own privacy practices.

No accounts or tracking

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No developer-operated backend is used.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry SDK is included.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or advertising identifier is requested.
  • The core app works without network access.

Your control

You can delete projects and profiles in LabelCal. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Files already saved or shared remain at the destination you selected.
