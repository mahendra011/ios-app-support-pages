Spin the wheel. Keep the choices yours.

Rouvelle is an offline roulette game for two adults sharing one iPhone. Pick a heat level, choose the categories, and spin through a complete private session.

Start a game

Both players confirm they are adults, set the heat ceiling, choose the prompt families, and agree to the shared rules. Tap the wheel or the Spin button to begin.

Stay in control

Pass replaces the current reveal. Softer lowers the heat ceiling. Pause freezes the game, while End closes the active session and opens Afterglow.

Everything is included

The paid App Store download includes all five heat levels, every journey, and the full reviewed prompt set. Rouvelle has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Works offline

Rouvelle needs no account or internet connection. It contains no ads, analytics, tracking, backend, or remote prompt service.

Reset local preferences

Open Settings and choose Erase local preferences. Rouvelle closes the current session and resets the haptics setting. The app does not save answers or revealed prompts.

For two adults in private. A wheel result is a suggestion. Either person can pass, go softer, pause, or end the game.
