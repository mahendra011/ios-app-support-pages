Privacy policy

Effective 25 August 2026.

No data collection

Rouvelle does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, backend, or remote content service.

Session data

Your heat level, category choices, current prompt, and session progress stay in memory on your device. Rouvelle clears them when you close the session or quit the app.

Local preference

Rouvelle stores one non-sensitive preference on the device: whether haptics are enabled. You can erase it from Settings.

App Store purchase

Apple handles the paid download. Rouvelle does not receive or store payment details and contains no in-app purchases.

Device features

Rouvelle requests no protected permissions. It covers the active screen in the app switcher to reduce accidental exposure.

Backups and deletion

Rouvelle does not create a prompt history. The haptics preference may be included in an operating-system backup. Use Erase local preferences or uninstall the app to remove its local container, subject to your device backup settings.
