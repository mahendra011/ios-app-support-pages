Twofold Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026.

Twofold does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, chat, or backend.

Private choices

Each partner's choices are processed in memory on the shared device. When the mutual overlap is created, individual answers are erased. Unmatched choices are not shown or saved.

Optional shared patterns

Lifetime users may explicitly save a shared pattern. It contains only mutual modes, shared time, energy, and pace—not either partner's private answers. It stays in the app's protected local container.

Purchases

Apple processes the lifetime in-app purchase and restoration under Apple's privacy practices. Twofold does not receive or store your payment details.

Deleting data

Use Erase everything inside Twofold to remove current state and any saved shared pattern. Uninstalling the app also removes its local container, subject to your operating-system backup settings.
