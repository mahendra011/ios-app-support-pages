FrameSpec Support

FrameSpec is a private mat and frame cut planner for preparing framing dimensions before ordering or cutting materials.

Common Questions

  • FrameSpec stores framing measurements locally on your device.
  • The app does not require an account, subscription, or internet connection.
  • Always verify rabbet depth, material thickness, and framer tolerances before ordering or cutting materials.
